# Multi-platform v0.5.9 candidate

First stage: public video posts and Reels/Shorts from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. All extraction and download operations are local using the existing engine and FFmpeg. No credentials or external backend. Public short/share links are passed to the engine for resolution. Multiple-entry social posts and private/login-required content are out of scope. Exact supported format and audio/video availability depends on the extractor. Only YouTube retains playlist mode. Existing Android share target accepts supported social links. The source URL is validated against an explicit HTTPS media-host and path allowlist, and thumbnails are restricted to platform CDNs.

The branch runs normal build, unit, signing, and emulator checks; release publication remains restricted to main. Before merging, run actual authorized public video fixture downloads on all four platforms (metadata, playable file, audio extraction, cancellation, failure paths), and review visual states. Unit tests do not establish that live third-party extraction succeeds. The previous v0.5.8 engine/init and startup failures remain a release blocker until resolved.
